# ❓ 10 Strategic Questions Before a Real Build

1. **Source of truth:** which system owns the lead and owner records today, and how often do they sync (CRM, portal, community app)?
2. **Consent provenance:** how was existing email, SMS, and WhatsApp consent captured, and can we evidence it with timestamps?
3. **Identity keys:** what is the most reliable match key across portals, Meta forms, CRM, and the community app (email, phone, national ID)?
4. **Pipeline size:** what is the real size of the qualified, consented base, and what monthly lead flow can paid media sustain?
5. **Definition of "booked visit":** what exactly counts (confirmed by agent, calendar event, attended), and who records it?
6. **Sales capacity:** how many agents and visit slots per day exist, and what is the lead assignment rule today?
7. **Owner vs lead boundary:** when does a lead become an owner in the data (reservation, contract, first payment), and who may contact each?
8. **Messaging governance:** who approves cross-sell offers to existing owners, and are there contact-frequency limits across business units?
9. **Channel readiness:** are WhatsApp Business and SMS sender IDs approved and registered for Egypt, and is Arabic RTL template approval needed?
10. **Success and budget:** what conversion rate and cost per visit make this worthwhile, and is there a holdout group allowed to measure true lift?
